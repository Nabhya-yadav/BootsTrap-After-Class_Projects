Best Place To Visit

Nizwa

Nizwa: Oman's ancient cultural heartland. It is home to the imposing 17th-century Nizwa Fort and a vibrant traditional souq. If you visit early on a Friday morning, you can witness the famous, chaotic live goat auction.

Muscat

Jebel Akhdar: The "Green Mountain" offers a cooler escape from the desert heat. It features luxury mountain resorts, rugged hiking trails, and terraced farmlands that burst with blooming Damask roses.
