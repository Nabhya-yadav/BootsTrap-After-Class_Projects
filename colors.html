Colors

Light blue

Light blue represents tranquility, trust, and openness. It is a soothing color that mimics the sky and shallow water.

Dark Blue

Dark blue represents power, professionalism, and deep intellect. It is a grounding color that mimics the night sky and the deep ocean.

Yellow

Happiness, optimism, sunshine, and intellect. It can also signify caution or deceit in certain contexts.

Red

Passion, energy, love, and desire. It also represents danger, urgency, and caution.

White

Purity, innocence, cleanliness, and peace. In some Eastern cultures, it represents death and mourning.
